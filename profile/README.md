# About NexarisCommunity
NexarisCommunity is an invite-only community that supports various products.
Not only do managers provide development support, but members who share common interests can also actively collaborate on development.
You can create a wide variety of products, ranging from useful tool apps to fully functional, high-security financial management apps.
Our future goals include publishing these products or operating them as company products.
If you would like to join, please apply via email or through our website.
We warmly welcome a wide range of individuals, from beginners with no prior development experience to seasoned creators who have built numerous products.
