# About NexarisCommunity
NexarisCommunity is an invite-only community that supports various products.<br>
Not only do managers provide development support, but members who share common interests can also actively collaborate on development.<br>
You can create a wide variety of products, ranging from useful tool apps to fully functional, high-security financial management apps.<br>
Our future goals include publishing these products or operating them as company products.<br>
If you would like to join, please apply via email or through our website.<br>
We warmly welcome a wide range of individuals, from beginners with no prior development experience to seasoned creators who have built numerous products.<br>
